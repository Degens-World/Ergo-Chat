Who are we?

We are a team of 2 members, cryptocurrency enthusiasts and Ergo is one of our favorite coins! We are full-time students who like to develop websites that helps other people with their problems!

What is Minergo?

You can already infer it from the name Minergo, Mine & Ergo. Minergo ensures that the miner is assigned the appropriate miner software with the correct overclocking settings for the respective video card(s). We will also focus on statistics and real-time charts of the known pools used to mine Ergo.

Why?

We experienced problems with the giant community spreadsheet as you cannot filter it, cannot read it easily and so we will simplify this with a straightforward UI for the user. This will be a big part of our data in the beginning but in the long run the miner can add data himself by filling in a form and this will be verified by a team in order to give the user a worry free experience. We saw that there is still the question "which pool should I use? What are the best overclocking settings for x GPU?" and we want to minimize this using our web tool!
